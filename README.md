🛒 BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

A Business Intelligence project focused on grocery sales performance, customer satisfaction, and outlet analysis using Microsoft Power BI.

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes BlinkIT grocery sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand sales performance, customer ratings, product categories, and outlet-level trends.

The dashboard transforms raw data into an interactive business report using key performance indicators (KPIs), data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and visualizations.

The primary objective is to provide a clear overview of grocery sales performance and help stakeholders explore data-driven business questions.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall grocery sales performance.
- Evaluate sales across different grocery item categories.
- Compare sales by product fat content.
- Understand sales distribution across outlet sizes and locations.
- Analyze sales by outlet type and establishment year.
- Monitor average sales, item counts, and customer ratings.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

💼 Business Questions

This dashboard is designed to explore questions such as:

- Which grocery item types contribute most to total sales?
- How do sales vary across fat-content categories?
- How does sales performance differ across outlet sizes?
- How are sales distributed across outlet locations?
- How do sales vary by outlet establishment year?
- How do average sales, item counts, and customer ratings differ by outlet type?
